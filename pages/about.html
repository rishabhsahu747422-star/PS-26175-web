About DepthWizard

An independent scientific research platform focused on democratizing 3D elevation data extraction from ubiquitous optical satellite and aerial imagery.

Mission & Core Objectives

Elevation models (DSMs and DEMs) form the bedrock of flood disaster mitigation, structural urban planning, telecom line-of-sight propagation, and tactical reconnaissance. Traditional sensor-heavy approaches (stereo imagery, LiDAR, InSAR) cannot scale fast enough to meet real-time emergency needs.

DepthWizard bridges this gap by proving that single optical satellite passes can yield reliable metric elevations and immersive 3D terrain meshes using modern Vision Transformers and semantic calibration.

Expected Deliverables & Software Suite

1. Elevation Estimation Engine

A high-performance Python/FastAPI module that accepts single optical imagery (GeoTIFF, PNG, JPG) and exports calibrated 16-bit Digital Surface Models (DSMs) in standard geospatial formats.

2. Interactive 3D Platform

A browser-deployable WebGL/Three.js viewer enabling users to fly through reconstructed terrain in first-person, inspect spot elevations, generate 2D transects, and toggle confidence overlays.
