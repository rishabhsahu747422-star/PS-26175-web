Datasets & Experimental Benchmarks

DepthWizard relies entirely on open-access, zero-cost remote sensing datasets for backbone fine-tuning, scale calibration, and real-world cross-terrain generalization.

Training Backbone 11,507 Tiles

GAMUS Dataset

Provider: Earthflow (Hugging Face)
Modality: Optical RGB + nDSM + 6-class semantic masks
Coverage: 5 US urban metropolitan centers

Provides the essential paired data to adapt Depth Anything V2 from natural perspective imagery to nadir satellite viewing geometry.

Hugging Face: earthflow/GAMUS →
Scale Anchoring 1 Arc-Sec (~30m)

SRTM 30m Global DEM

Provider: NASA / USGS / OpenTopography
Modality: Radar Topography Elevation Grid
Coverage: Near-global landmass (56°S to 60°N)

Provides the reference coarse elevation trends to anchor scale-agnostic relative depth into true real-world metric elevation without requiring local ground survey teams.

OpenTopography Portal →
Generalization 10m Optical

Copernicus Sentinel-2

Provider: European Space Agency (ESA)
Modality: MSI Multispectral Optical Bands (RGB)
Coverage: Global 5-day revisit

Used to qualitatively evaluate real-world transfer over dense Indian metropolitan areas (Delhi, Mumbai) and high-relief Himalayan mountain terrain.

Evaluation Metrics & Benchmarking Protocol

Quantitative accuracy assessment against high-resolution airborne LiDAR reference data.

Evaluation Metric Mathematical Definition Target Criterion Observed Impact
RMSE (Root Mean Square Error) √[(1/M) ∑ (H_pred - H_gt)²] Penalizes large outlier errors on tall structures Building height error reduced by ~44%
MAE (Mean Absolute Error) (1/M) ∑ |H_pred - H_gt| Measures average elevation discrepancy Tree canopy error reduced by ~35%
Pearson Correlation (r) Cov(H_pred, H_gt) / (σ_p • σ_g) Measures topographical trend fidelity Maintains stable correlation across urban & hilly terrain
