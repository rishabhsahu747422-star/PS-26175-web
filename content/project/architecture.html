System Architecture & Data Topology

Decoupled data pipelines, ingestion microservices, and evidentiary formats

1. High-Level Architectural Topology

TRACEOIL adopts a modular, event-driven architecture designed to decouple asynchronous earth observation satellite downlinks from real-time maritime AIS transponder processing and compute-intensive hydrodynamic reverse particle simulations.

Click to view fullscreen

2. Core Subsystems

Satellite Ingestion Worker

Polls Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem via OData API, downloads Sentinel-1 GRD and Sentinel-2 L2A tiles for specified geographic AOIs.

Detection & Segmentation

Applies radiometric calibration, Lee speckle filtering, and neural segmentation to isolate dark formation slick polygons and CFAR ship targets.

Attribution Engine

Integrates reverse Lagrangian drift modeling with historical AIS waypoint corridors to calculate vessel attribution probability vectors.

3. Data Formats & Interoperability

All internal modules exchange standard geospatial and scientific formats:

  • Raw Imagery: Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFF (COG) with embedded radiometric metadata and coordinate reference system (EPSG:4326).
  • Hydrodynamic Fields: NetCDF-4 structured grids for ocean currents (u, v) and GRIB2 for 10m wind fields.
  • Extracted Features: GeoJSON FeatureCollections for slick polygons, centerlines, and vessel bounding boxes.
  • Evidentiary Dossier: Digitally signed PDF report accompanied by open GeoJSON spatial trajectory tracks.
