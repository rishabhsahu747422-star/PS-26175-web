4. Evaluation & Roadmap

System Limitations & Boundary Conditions

Wind speed operational envelopes, revisit rates, and AIS transponder disabling

1. Wind Speed Operational Envelope

SAR oil spill observation is governed by a strict physical wind window:

Low Wind Threshold (< 2.5 m/s)

At wind speeds below 2.5 m/s, zero Bragg capillary waves form on the sea surface. The entire ocean appears dark, creating severe false alarms.

High Wind Threshold (> 14 m/s)

At wind speeds exceeding 14 m/s, intense wave breaking and whitecapping physically disperse the oil film into the water column, dissipating the surface signature.

2. Operational Constraints

  • Constellation Revisit Cadence: Sentinel-1 revisit intervals over Indian EEZ range from 6 to 12 days, leaving temporal surveillance gaps.
  • AIS Non-Compliance: Rogue vessels can physically power down AIS transponders ("going dark") prior to bilge dumping.
  • Turbulent Coastal Surf Zones: Shallow bathymetry and breaking surf zones degrade open-ocean Lagrangian drift assumptions within 2 nautical miles of coastlines.
