4. Evaluation & Roadmap

Evaluation Metrics & Benchmark Analysis

Benchmarking protocols, IoU metrics, F1-scores, and false alarm analysis

1. Semantic Segmentation Evaluation Metrics

Dark formation segmentation accuracy is evaluated using standard pixel-level computer vision metrics:

  • Intersection over Union (IoU): Measuring geometric overlap between predicted slick mask and ground truth.
  • Precision & Recall: Measuring false positive rate (lookalikes mistaken for oil) and false negative rate (missed slicks).
  • F1-Score: Harmonic mean balancing precision and sensitivity.

2. Attribution & Drift Metrics

Hydrodynamic reverse drift precision is measured by:

  • Spatial Intercept Error (Delta s): Orthogonal metric distance in meters between reconstructed discharge origin and ground-truth vessel trajectory.
  • Temporal Intercept Delta (Delta t): Time difference in minutes between simulated release and AIS passage timestamp.
  • Top-1 & Top-3 Attribution Accuracy: Percentage of trials where the true delinquent vessel is ranked first in the attribution matrix.
