4. Evaluation & Roadmap

Future Directions & Research Roadmap

Hyperspectral integration, PINN physics drift modeling, and command-center webhooks

1. Hyperspectral Chemical Fingerprinting

Future phases will integrate next-generation spaceborne hyperspectral sensors (e.g., Italian PRISMA and German EnMAP). By measuring narrow absorption features across the 400–2500 nm range, the system can estimate slick thickness and distinguish light diesel fuels from heavy crude emulsions.

2. Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)

To overcome spatial resolution limits of numerical ocean models in coastal straits, future work will couple Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) directly with shallow-water Navier-Stokes equations to achieve sub-kilometer drift accuracy.

3. Direct Maritime Command Integration

Developing automated real-time alert webhooks to interface with Indian Coast Guard Maritime Rescue Coordination Centres (MRCC) for immediate intercept tasking.
