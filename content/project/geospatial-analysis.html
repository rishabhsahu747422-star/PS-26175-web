Geospatial Analysis & MetOcean Forcing

Copernicus CMEMS ocean currents, ECMWF wind fields, and spatial coordinate projections

1. MetOcean Environmental Forcing Data

Reverse drift accuracy is directly tied to the fidelity of oceanic surface current and atmospheric wind forcing models:

Click to view fullscreen
  • Copernicus CMEMS: Global Analysis Forecast product delivering hourly surface eastward (u) and northward (v) currents at 1/12° resolution.
  • ECMWF 10m Winds: Hourly surface wind vector components driving the surface leeway shear layer.

2. Spatial Coordinate Transformations

Raw Sentinel-1 orbit geometry is projected from SAR slant range to ground range (EPSG:4326 WGS84 geographic coordinates). For local distance and drift computations, coordinates are dynamically reprojected to the corresponding Universal Transverse Mercator (UTM) zone (e.g., UTM Zone 43N for Western Indian Coastline) to preserve metric distances.
