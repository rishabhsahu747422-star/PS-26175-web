2. Mathematical Operations Per Stage

Each stage executes mathematically verified transformations:

  1. Radiometric Calibration: Converting digital numbers to calibrated physical backscatter coefficient.
  2. Speckle Suppression: 7x7 Refined Lee filter preserving edge boundaries while reducing granular speckle variance.
  3. Adaptive Segmentation: Moving spatial threshold isolating capillary wave suppression.
  4. CFAR Bright Target Extraction: Constant False Alarm Rate hypothesis test for metallic ship corner reflectors.
  5. Lagrangian Reverse Advection: Integrating reverse trajectory with ocean currents and wind leeway.
  6. AIS Track Correlation: Temporal and spatial distance weighting producing attribution probability vectors.
