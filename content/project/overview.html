Project Overview & Research Mission

High-level executive summary, mission objectives, and operational scope for SIH 2026

1. Executive Summary

TRACEOIL is an open-access research initiative developed under the Smart India Hackathon 2026 framework. The project addresses the critical global challenge of marine oil pollution through spaceborne remote sensing and automated vessel attribution.

By bridging European Space Agency (ESA) Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery, Sentinel-2 multi-spectral optical data, hydrodynamic ocean surface current models, and global Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel transponder feeds, TRACEOIL provides an end-to-end framework capable of detecting surface discharges, classifying slicks against biogenic lookalikes, and tracking discharge origin corridors back in time to identify delinquent commercial vessels.

2. Smart India Hackathon 2026 Context

As maritime traffic across the Indian Ocean Region (IOR) intensifies—with over 100,000 commercial vessels traversing the Malacca Strait and Arabian Sea routes annually—sovereign coastal surveillance is of paramount strategic and ecological priority.

3. Research Scope & Deliverables

The TRACEOIL research framework delivers five core components:

  • Automated Satellite Ingestion: Scheduled scraping and indexing of Sentinel-1 C-Band SAR products covering Indian coastal corridors.
  • Dark Formation Semantic Segmentation: Deep learning model for detecting capillary wave-damped oil slicks with low false-alarm rates.
  • Ship Extraction Engine: Constant False Alarm Rate (CFAR) radar backscatter detector identifying maritime vessel positions.
  • Lagrangian Reverse Drift Modeling: Hydrodynamic particle trajectory simulation forced by Copernicus CMEMS current vectors and ECMWF 10m wind fields.
  • Forensic Evidentiary Reporting: Automated generation of cryptographically timestamped attribution reports for legal enforcement.
