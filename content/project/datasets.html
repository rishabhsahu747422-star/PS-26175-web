Dataset Documentation: GAMUS, SRTM & Sentinel-2

Comprehensive technical specifications of training, calibration, and validation datasets.

1. GAMUS Dataset (Earthflow)

The primary foundation for monocular depth fine-tuning. Comprises 11,507 paired tiles (512x512) across 5 US metropolitan areas, featuring high-resolution aerial RGB imagery, normalized DSM ground truth from airborne LiDAR, and 6-class land-cover semantic masks.

2. SRTM 30m Global DEM

Shuttle Radar Topography Mission 1 arc-second dataset provided via NASA/USGS and OpenTopography. Provides seamless macro-topographical elevation trends across global coordinates.

3. Copernicus Sentinel-2 MSI

10m multispectral optical imagery used to evaluate real-world transfer over complex Indian geographical landscapes, ensuring stable model performance outside the training domain.

DEPTHWIZARD RESEARCH PLATFORM

Independent scientific research project advancing single-view optical height estimation, per-land-cover scale calibration, and real-time 3D terrain flythrough.
