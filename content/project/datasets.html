1. Data Sources & Sensor Specifications

TRACEOIL integrates satellite observations, metocean models, and vessel tracking broadcasts:

Dataset Sensor / Constellation Band / Polarization Resolution Provider
Sentinel-1 GRD C-Band Synthetic Aperture Radar VV + VH Dual-Pol 10m ESA Copernicus
Sentinel-2 L2A Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI) B2, B3, B4, B8 10m – 20m ESA Copernicus
AIS Streams VHF Maritime Transceivers NMEA 0183 Messages Sub-minute updates MarineCadastre / Spire
Copernicus CMEMS Numerical Ocean Model Surface Velocity (u, v) 1/12° (~8km) Mercator Ocean

2. M4D ITI Oil Spill Benchmark

For algorithm validation, TRACEOIL utilizes the M4D ITI Oil Spill Detection Dataset, containing verified Sentinel-1 SAR crops labeled across 5 semantic classes: Sea Surface, Oil Spill, Lookalike, Ship, and Land.
