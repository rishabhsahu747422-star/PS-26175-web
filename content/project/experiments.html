Experimental Setup & Simulation Scenarios

Experimental test matrix, simulation scenarios, and calibration runs

1. Experimental Design & Methodology

To rigorously evaluate TRACEOIL without relying on synthetic or fabricated test data, our experimental protocol tests detection and attribution across three controlled scenarios:

Scenario A: Known Benchmarks

Validating dark slick semantic segmentation on public CERTH-ITI annotated Sentinel-1 crops.

Scenario B: Synthetic Drift

Injecting virtual slick parcels at known vessel AIS locations, advecting forward, and testing backward reconstruction error.

Scenario C: Historical Case Studies

Backtesting against historical verified maritime spill events documented by international coast guards.
