About the TRACEOIL Research Initiative

Developed by our undergraduate research cohort for Smart India Hackathon 2026. Dedicated to autonomous oceanic environmental protection and spaceborne maritime surveillance.

1. Hackathon & Problem Context

TRACEOIL is formulated as a response to real-world maritime operational challenges under the Smart India Hackathon 2026 framework. The project addresses the critical gap between early satellite detection of marine pollution and prompt, legally viable vessel attribution.
